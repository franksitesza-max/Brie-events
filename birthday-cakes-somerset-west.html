Birthday cakes in Somerset West

A custom birthday cake starts with a clear brief.

Polite Ndoro makes birthday cakes around your occasion, colours, theme, size request, flavour choice, and budget. Send the key details first so she can check the date and quote the right design.

Ask Polite for a quote See real cakes

Send these details with your first message

  • Event date and preferred handover time
  • Birthday person's age and the exact name or message
  • Guest count and the way you plan to cut and serve the cake
  • Theme, colours, finish, and inspiration images
  • Preferred flavour and any allergy or dietary request
  • Budget and whether you need collection or delivery

Notice period and availability

Standard items need at least one week of notice. Custom birthday cakes are best requested about two weeks ahead. Detailed themes, handmade toppers, busy dates, and delivery can need more planning. A message does not reserve the date; Polite secures the order after you accept the quote and pay the required deposit.

Size and servings

Guest count gives Polite a starting point. Cake height, diameter, tiers, filling, and portion style affect the number of servings. Tell her whether children or adults will receive dessert portions and whether other treats will sit on the table. She will confirm the suitable size in the quote instead of relying on a generic serving chart.

From inspiration to your own cake

Use screenshots and photographs to show the mood, colour, piping, or topper style you like. Polite makes a handmade interpretation for your order. Screen colours, ingredient availability, and handmade details can create small differences from a reference image.

Current starting prices

Published examples start at R550 for a small cake, R650 for a medium cake, R800 for a large cake, and R1,200 for a two-tier cake. A bento cake with cupcakes starts at R350. Design detail, decorations, flavour, timing, and handover arrangements affect the final quote.
