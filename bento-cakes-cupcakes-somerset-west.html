Bento cakes and cupcakes

A smaller format can still feel made for the occasion.

Brie Cakes offers bento cake and cupcake combinations from R350. The format suits gifts, small birthdays, and celebrations that need a personal message without a large centrepiece.

Ask Polite for a quote See real cakes

Choose a bento set when

  • You want a compact cake with matching cupcakes
  • The celebration has a small guest list
  • You need a personal gift with a name, age, or short message
  • You want separate portions alongside a small cake

What the R350 starting point means

A bento cake with cupcakes starts from R350. The final quote depends on the number of cupcakes, flavour request, colours, piping, message, toppers, decoration, timing, and handover arrangement. Send the budget and reference image so Polite can confirm what fits.

Build a clear small-format brief

Choose one colour direction, one short message, and one focal detail. Check the spelling and date before you approve the quote. If the set is a surprise, tell Polite who will collect it and how the recipient's name should appear.

Flavours and dietary requests

Ask Polite which flavours and fillings are available for your date and format. Tell her about allergies or dietary requests before accepting the quote. A custom kitchen may handle common allergens, so the business cannot promise an allergen-free product unless Polite confirms it in writing.

Collection and transport

Keep the box level and cool. Clear a stable place in the vehicle and avoid direct sun. If you need delivery in Somerset West or another listed area, ask before payment so Polite can confirm the route and fee.

Ask for a bento quote

Send the date, occasion, message, colours, flavour request, cupcake quantity, inspiration image, and collection or delivery area to 068 553 3304.
