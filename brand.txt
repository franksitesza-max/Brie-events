# Brie Events brand reference

Lang: en-ZA

Official business name: Brie Events
Primary customer-facing name: Brie Cakes
Accepted alternate name: Brie Events Cakes and More

## Naming

Use "Brie Cakes" in customer-facing references about cakes. Use "Brie Events (Brie Cakes)" when a legal or identity distinction helps the reader. Do not treat the names as separate businesses.

## Description

Brie Events is a Somerset West local business marketed as Brie Cakes. Polite Ndoro makes custom birthday cakes, celebration cakes, cupcakes, bento cakes, wedding and event cakes, and selected party treats.

## Voice

Use warm, direct South African English. State prices as starting points and refer customers to Polite for a written quote. Avoid claims such as best, cheapest, guaranteed, allergen-free, or exact replica unless the business confirms them for a specific order.

## Visual identity

Use the official round floral cake logo from https://brieevents.co.za/assets/brie-events-logo-and-favicon.jpg without changing its proportions. The website uses blush pink, berry, cocoa, cream, and warm white.
