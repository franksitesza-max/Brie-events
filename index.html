Cake order questions

Answers about prices, notice, delivery, allergens, and ordering.

Where can I order a birthday cake in Somerset West?

You can order a custom birthday cake from Brie Cakes by sending Polite your event date, theme, flavor, colors, size, and inspiration photos on WhatsApp.

Can Brie Cakes bake a custom birthday cake?

Yes. Polite bakes custom birthday cakes around your occasion, theme, colors, flavor, and budget. Send your idea on WhatsApp for a custom quote.

Does Brie Cakes make kids birthday cakes and themed cakes?

Yes. Brie Cakes makes personalised kids birthday cakes and themed birthday cakes, including character-inspired designs, colors, age details, and party themes.

Can I order cakes for Stellenbosch or Cape Town events?

Selected Stellenbosch and Cape Town orders are available depending on the date, design, notice period, and delivery requirements. Ask Polite when you request a quote.

How early should I order a cake?

Standard items need at least 1 week notice. Custom cakes are best ordered 2 weeks ahead so there is time to confirm design details and decorations.

Why do cake prices vary?

Each cake is made to order. Size, flavor, design detail, theme, decorations, and timing all affect the final price.
