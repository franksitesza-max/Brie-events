Cake order terms

Terms of Service

These terms apply to cake and party-treat orders that Polite Ndoro accepts for Brie Events, marketed as Brie Cakes. Your written quote may add order-specific details.

Effective date: 11 August 2026

1. Business and contact details

Polite Ndoro owns and operates Brie Events from Somerset West, Western Cape. Brie Cakes is the customer-facing cake brand.

  • Email: politendoro@gmail.com
  • Telephone and WhatsApp: +27 68 553 3304

2. Enquiries, quotes, and acceptance

A website visit, message, or availability discussion does not create an order. Polite will prepare a quote from the information you provide. The quote records its validity period, price, deposit, balance, handover method, and agreed cake brief.

You accept the order when you approve the quote and Polite receives the required deposit. If the deposit arrives after the quote expires, Polite may need to confirm the price and date again.

3. Deposit and payment

Brie Events requires a 50% deposit to secure an accepted order. The written quote states the amount and payment deadline. You must pay the balance by the date in the quote. Polite may pause work or withhold handover if an agreed payment remains due, subject to your rights under South African law.

4. Final cake brief

You must check the event date, spelling, age, message, flavour, size, colours, theme, decorations, collection or delivery details, and allergy information before approving the quote. Tell Polite about an error as soon as you find it.

Inspiration images guide the style. Polite will create her own handmade interpretation and cannot promise an exact copy, exact colour match across screens and materials, or identical placement of handmade details.

5. Changes and substitutions

Ask for a change in writing. Polite will confirm whether the schedule and materials allow it. A change may affect the price or handover time. Brie Events may use a close substitute when a decoration or ingredient becomes unavailable, after discussing a material change with you where time permits.

6. Postponement and cancellation

Tell Polite in writing if you want to postpone or cancel. Brie Events will assess the notice period, work completed, ingredients and custom decorations bought, supplier charges, and the chance of filling the reserved date.

The business may deduct a fair cancellation charge from money received where the Consumer Protection Act permits it. Any refund will reflect work and costs that the order caused. These terms do not remove a remedy that South African consumer law gives you.

If Brie Events cannot fulfil an accepted order, Polite will offer a suitable change or refund the money received for the unfulfilled order. You choose whether to accept a proposed replacement.

7. Collection and delivery

Collection takes place at the date, time, and location that Polite confirms. The person collecting should inspect the cake before leaving. Responsibility for safe transport passes to you or your chosen courier after handover.

Delivery serves selected areas by prior arrangement. The quote will state the address, fee, and delivery window. You must provide access, a working contact number, and a person who can receive the order. Extra waiting, redelivery, or address changes may add a charge if Polite confirms it with you.

8. Storage, transport, and handover care

Keep the cake level, supported, cool, and away from direct sun, heaters, pets, and strong smells. Use the storage instructions supplied for the order. Decorations, buttercream, chocolate, and toppers can react to heat, moisture, movement, and refrigeration.

Brie Events remains responsible for defects that existed at handover. The business cannot take responsibility for damage caused after handover by unsafe transport, storage, display, handling, weather, or another service provider.

9. Allergens and dietary requests

Tell Polite about allergies and dietary requests before accepting the quote. Ask for ingredient information that matters to the customer or guest. A custom kitchen may handle common allergens. Brie Events cannot guarantee an allergen-free product unless Polite confirms that requirement in writing for the order.

10. Customer-supplied content and intellectual property

You confirm that Brie Events may use the names, messages, logos, photographs, and design references you supply to complete your order. You remain responsible for having permission to use that material.

Brie Events owns its website copy, original photographs, logo, and original design work. You may share photographs of your purchased cake for personal use. Commercial copying or reuse requires written permission.

11. Cake photographs and publicity

Polite may photograph the finished cake. Brie Events will ask before publishing a customer, child, private event, personal message, or customer-supplied photograph. You may request removal under the Privacy Policy.

12. Website and external services

The website provides general information and starting prices. The accepted quote controls your order. Links to WhatsApp, Facebook, email, maps, or other providers open services that Brie Events does not control.

13. Complaints and governing law

Contact Polite with the order details and photographs if something is wrong. Give the business a fair chance to inspect the issue and propose a remedy. South African law governs these terms, including the Consumer Protection Act and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act where they apply.
