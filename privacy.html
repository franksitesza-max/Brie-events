Privacy at Brie Events

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Polite Ndoro, trading as Brie Events and marketing the cake business as Brie Cakes, handles personal information under South African data protection law.

Effective date: 11 August 2026

1. Responsible party and Information Officer

Polite Ndoro is the owner, responsible party, and Information Officer for Brie Events. Brie Cakes is the customer-facing cake brand. The business operates from Somerset West, Western Cape.

  • Email: politendoro@gmail.com
  • Telephone and WhatsApp: +27 68 553 3304

Use these details for privacy requests, corrections, objections, deletion requests, or complaints about the way the business handles your information.

2. Information you choose to provide

Brie Events receives information when you contact Polite through WhatsApp, email, telephone, or Facebook. This may include your name, contact details, event date, delivery or collection details, cake brief, flavour and size requests, budget, allergy or dietary information, inspiration images, payment records, and correspondence.

Please avoid sending identity numbers, financial passwords, medical records, or other information that the order does not require. If a cake includes a child's name, age, or photograph, send only the detail needed for the agreed design.

3. Why the business uses this information

Polite uses personal information to answer an enquiry, check availability, prepare a quote, confirm a design, manage payment, make and hand over the order, resolve a complaint, keep business records, and meet duties under South African law.

Brie Events does not sell customer information. Polite will contact you about your enquiry or order. The business will ask for consent before sending separate direct-marketing messages.

4. Website cookies and technical records

This website has no contact form, customer account, checkout, advertising tracker, or analytics service. Brie Events does not set website cookies.

Vercel hosts the site and may process technical request data such as an IP address, browser details, requested URL, timestamp, and security logs to deliver and protect the website. Google Fonts supplies type files when your browser loads a page. Those providers handle technical data under their own privacy terms.

5. Service providers and cross-border processing

Brie Events uses Vercel for hosting, Google services for email and fonts, and Meta services for WhatsApp and Facebook contact. These companies may process information outside South Africa. Polite limits shared information to what the service or order requires and uses established providers with published privacy and security terms.

When you follow a link to WhatsApp, Facebook, Gmail, or another external service, that provider's terms and privacy policy apply to your use of its platform.

6. Customer and gallery photographs

Polite may photograph a completed cake for business records. Brie Events will publish customer-supplied photographs, event photographs, names, or identifying details only with permission. A customer may withdraw gallery permission or request a takedown through the contact details above. Polite will remove the material from channels under the business's control after checking the request.

7. Retention and deletion

Brie Events keeps information for the time needed to answer the enquiry, complete the order, handle a dispute, support food-safety records, keep accounting records, or meet a legal duty. Polite reviews old records and deletes or de-identifies information that the business no longer needs. Backup and platform copies may take time to expire.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask whether Brie Events holds your personal information. You may request access, correction, deletion, or proof of consent. You may object to processing or withdraw consent where consent provides the basis for use. Some accounting, dispute, food-safety, or legal records may need to remain in place.

Polite may ask for enough information to confirm your identity before acting on a request. This protects customer records from disclosure to the wrong person.

9. Security and incidents

Polite uses access controls, device security, limited collection, and trusted service providers to protect customer information. No system removes every risk. If a security compromise creates a risk to you, Brie Events will investigate, contain the problem, preserve required records, and give notices required by POPIA.

10. Complaints and policy changes

Send a privacy complaint to Polite first so the business can investigate it. You may also contact the Information Regulator South Africa.

Brie Events may update this policy when its services, providers, or legal duties change. The effective date at the top records the current version.

POPIA-aligned notice

This policy supports Brie Events' POPIA responsibilities. Compliance also depends on day-to-day security, retention, access control, incident response, and staff conduct.
