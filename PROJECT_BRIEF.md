# Brie Cakes Website Strategy & Design Brief

Prepared by Frank Sites

## Overview

Brie Cakes is a local custom cake business owned by Polite Ndoro. While the business offers a variety of baked goods, event treats, and catering services, the website should primarily position Brie Cakes as a trusted birthday cake specialist.

The goal is not to build a large bakery website with dozens of pages and products.

The goal is to create a beautiful, trustworthy, and personal online presence that helps visitors:

- Understand what Brie Cakes offers
- View examples of previous work
- See rough pricing information
- Learn about Polite
- Contact Brie Cakes through WhatsApp

The website should feel human, welcoming, and professional rather than corporate or overly commercial.

## Brand Positioning

Primary positioning:

Custom birthday cakes for every celebration

Secondary services:

- Celebration cakes
- Cupcakes
- Dessert treats
- Wedding cakes
- Event cakes
- Corporate orders

Visitors should immediately associate Brie Cakes with birthdays and celebrations.

The website can mention other services, but birthday cakes should remain the primary focus throughout the site.

## Target Audience

Primary audience:

- Parents planning children's birthday parties
- Adults celebrating birthdays
- Families planning special celebrations

Secondary audience:

- Wedding clients
- Corporate clients
- Event planners
- Baby showers and special occasions

## Brand Personality

The website should feel:

- Warm
- Friendly
- Personal
- Feminine
- Elegant
- Trustworthy
- Creative
- Approachable

Avoid:

- Corporate feeling
- Luxury for the sake of luxury
- Overly playful childish design
- Generic bakery templates
- Ecommerce marketplace feeling

## Website Structure

The website should be a single-page website.

No additional pages are necessary.

Navigation should simply scroll to sections.

Navigation links:

- Home
- Cakes
- Pricing
- About
- Gallery
- Contact

## Website Sections

### 1. Hero Section

Purpose:

Immediately communicate what Brie Cakes does.

Content:

- Logo
- Headline placeholder: write a short welcoming headline focused on custom cakes and celebrations
- Subheading placeholder: write 2 to 3 personal sentences introducing Brie Cakes
- Primary button: WhatsApp Order Button
- Secondary button: View Gallery

WhatsApp button requirements:

The button must open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message.

Suggested message:

Hi Polite, I'm interested in ordering a cake from Brie Cakes. My event date is [date] and I would like a cake for [occasion]. Please let me know if you are available.

### 2. Introduction Section

Purpose:

Briefly explain the business.

Copy placeholder:

Write a personal introduction about Brie Cakes, your approach to baking, and your focus on creating memorable celebration cakes.

This section should remain text-focused and personal.

No large cards or unnecessary visual elements.

### 3. What We Make

Purpose:

Clearly explain services.

Suggested categories:

- Birthday Cakes
- Celebration Cakes
- Cupcakes & Sweet Treats
- Wedding & Event Cakes
- Corporate Orders

This section should be simple and informative.

### 4. Rough Pricing Guide

Purpose:

Reduce unnecessary enquiries and provide budget expectations.

Introduction placeholder:

Write a short note explaining that prices vary depending on design, size, and detail.

Pricing examples:

- Small cakes: from R[Amount]
- Medium cakes: from R[Amount]
- Large custom cakes: from R[Amount]
- Cupcakes: from R[Amount] per dozen
- Dessert treats: from R[Amount]
- Wedding and event cakes: quoted individually

Disclaimer:

Final pricing depends on design requirements and order details.

### 5. About Polite Ndoro

Purpose:

Build trust.

This should be one of the most important sections on the website.

Include:

- Professional photograph
- Name: Polite Ndoro
- Bio placeholder: write Polite's story, experience, passion for baking, and commitment to customer service

Visitors should feel like they are ordering from a real person rather than a business logo.

### 6. Gallery

Purpose:

Allow photography to sell the product.

Copy placeholder:

Write a short introduction encouraging visitors to browse previous work.

Gallery categories:

- Birthday Cakes
- Children's Cakes
- Celebration Cakes
- Cupcakes
- Special Orders

Only use real photographs whenever possible.

### 7. How To Order

Purpose:

Simplify the ordering process.

Steps:

1. Send your cake idea
2. Share your preferred flavor, colors, and theme
3. Receive a quote and confirm your order
4. Collect your cake or arrange delivery

Keep this section extremely simple.

### 8. Business Hours

- Weekdays: 8am - 4pm
- Weekends: 9am - 3pm
- Standard items: minimum 1 week notice
- Custom cakes: recommended 2 weeks notice

### 9. Contact Section

Purpose:

Create a clear final call to action.

Heading placeholder:

Write a friendly invitation to get in touch.

Contact information:

- WhatsApp: [Phone Number]
- Phone: [Phone Number]
- Email: [Email Address]
- Instagram: [Instagram Handle]

Primary call to action:

WhatsApp Brie Cakes

Secondary call to action:

Send an Email

## Design Philosophy

The website should feel modern but personal.

The design should support the content rather than compete with it.

Text and information should be prioritized over decorative elements.

Avoid creating sections simply to fill space.

Every section must provide genuine value.

## Visual Style

Use:

- Soft colors
- Clean layouts
- Rounded corners
- Beautiful photography
- Consistent spacing
- Elegant typography
- Simple transitions

Avoid:

- Large empty spaces
- Overdesigned sections
- Excessive animations
- Unnecessary cards
- Floating decorative elements
- Glassmorphism
- Complex effects
- Generic AI-generated layouts

## Frank Sites Build Rules

These rules exist specifically to reduce revision requests and speed up development.

- Single-page website only
- No unnecessary pages
- No placeholder lorem ipsum
- Use bracket placeholders for unfinished copy
- No giant spacing between sections
- No empty containers
- No oversized hero sections
- Use one consistent content width
- Consistent padding throughout
- Consistent button styles
- Consistent border radius
- Consistent image styling
- Mobile-first design
- All sections aligned to one grid
- Keep layouts simple
- Prioritize information over decoration
- If unsure, choose simplicity

## Success Metric

A successful website should allow a visitor to:

1. Understand what Brie Cakes offers within 10 seconds
2. View examples of previous cakes
3. Understand approximate pricing
4. Learn who Polite Ndoro is
5. Contact Brie Cakes through WhatsApp within one click

If those five goals are achieved, the website has done its job.
