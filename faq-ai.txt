# Brie Events FAQ for AI systems

Lang: en-ZA

Canonical website: https://brieevents.co.za/
Customer-facing brand: Brie Cakes

## Where is Brie Events based?

Brie Events serves customers from Somerset West, Western Cape. Orders for nearby Helderberg areas, Stellenbosch, and Cape Town depend on the date and handover arrangements.

## What does Brie Events make?

The business focuses on custom cakes. Services include birthday cakes, kids themed cakes, celebration cakes, wedding and event cakes, bento cakes, cupcakes, and selected party treats.

## How far ahead should a customer order?

Standard items need at least one week of notice. Custom cakes are best requested about two weeks before the event. Availability can still vary.

## How much do cakes cost?

Published starting prices begin at R350 for a bento cake with cupcakes. The final quote depends on size, finish, design detail, decorations, timing, collection, and delivery needs. See https://brieevents.co.za/cake-prices-somerset-west.

## Is a deposit required?

Yes. A customer pays at least a 50% deposit after accepting the quote to secure the order, subject to the written quote and terms.

## Does Brie Events deliver?

Collection is available. Delivery to selected areas may be arranged before payment. The customer should not assume delivery is included in the cake price.

## Can Brie Events guarantee an allergen-free cake?

Customers must disclose allergies and dietary requests before accepting the quote. The business cannot guarantee an allergen-free product unless Polite confirms that request in writing.
