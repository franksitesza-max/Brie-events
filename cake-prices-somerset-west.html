Details that change the quote

  • Cake size, height, number of tiers, and requested servings
  • Flavour, filling, finish, and ingredient requirements
  • Piping, handmade figures, toppers, edible prints, and other decorations
  • Short notice, event date, and time needed for the design
  • Collection, delivery distance, and handover requirements

How to work with a budget

Share the budget at the start. Polite can then discuss format, scale, and which design features deserve the most attention. A clear priority such as colour, height, a name topper, or one themed focal point helps her shape the brief without guessing.

Deposit and date reservation

You pay at least a 50% deposit after accepting the written quote. The deposit secures the order under the quote and Terms of Service. A request for a price or date check does not reserve a production slot.

Get a useful quote

Send the event date, guest count, format, theme, flavour request, inspiration image, budget, and handover area to Polite on WhatsApp.
