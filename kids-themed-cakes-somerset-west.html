Kids themed cakes in Somerset West

Turn the party theme into a cake brief Polite can build.

Brie Cakes makes personalised children's birthday cakes with age details, colour palettes, piping, toppers, and character-inspired themes. A focused brief keeps the design readable and the quote clear.

Ask Polite for a quote See real cakes

Choose the details that carry the theme

Start with one main theme and two or three colours. Add the child's age, exact spelling of the name, and the message you want on the board or topper. If you have several references, mark the parts you like in each one so Polite can see your priorities.

Think about the whole party table

Tell Polite about the backdrop, table colours, plates, balloons, and other treats if you want the cake to fit the room. A bento cake or cupcakes may suit a small celebration. A larger or tiered cake can create more height for a bigger table.

Toppers, toys, and small parts

Ask which decorations are edible and which need removal before serving. Adults should remove non-edible toppers, supports, wires, and small parts before children receive a slice. Keep the cake and loose decorations away from unsupervised young children.

Use real Brie Cakes work as a reference

The homepage gallery includes real Barbie, pony, Cocomelon, princess, Badanamu, Paw Patrol, and Spiderman designs made by Brie Cakes. Use those photographs to discuss finish and layout. Polite will still adapt the final cake to your brief, budget, and available materials.

Book with enough notice

Custom cakes are best requested about two weeks before the party. Handmade theme elements can take time to source or prepare. Send the date first if the party falls on a busy weekend, then complete the design brief after Polite confirms availability.

A useful first WhatsApp

Send the party date, child's age, exact name, theme, colours, guest count, inspiration image, budget, and collection or delivery request to 068 553 3304.
