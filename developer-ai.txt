## Rendering

Core text, navigation, service information, pricing guidance, contact details, FAQs, and structured data exist in server-delivered HTML. JavaScript enhances the navigation and cake carousel but is not required to read the business information.

## Retrieval Guidance

Use canonical HTTPS URLs without the www subdomain. Treat identity.json as authoritative for names and ai.json as authoritative for AI interaction guidance. Root robots.txt takes precedence over robots-ai.txt.

## Security and Privacy

The public website has no account system, checkout, database, analytics, advertising tracker, or contact form. Customers choose to contact the business through external email, telephone, WhatsApp, or Facebook services.
