Areas discussed for orders

Brie Events is based in Somerset West and serves the Helderberg. Customers may ask about Strand, Gordon's Bay, Lwandle, Sir Lowry's Pass, Heldervue, Stellenbosch, and Cape Town. Availability depends on the date, cake design, distance, and delivery schedule.

Collection checklist

  • Confirm the collection date, time, and location in writing
  • Clear a flat space in the vehicle before you arrive
  • Use air conditioning and keep the cake out of direct sun
  • Place the box on a level floor or stable surface, not a seat
  • Drive with slow turns and gentle braking
  • Take the cake to its cool storage or display place after arrival

Delivery needs a confirmed arrangement

Delivery is not included unless the quote says so. Give Polite the complete address, a working contact number, access instructions, venue contact, and the time someone can receive the order. Address or timing changes can affect the route and fee.

Storage and display

Follow the storage instructions supplied for your cake. Keep it level, cool, and away from sun, heaters, pets, strong smells, and busy walkways. Some finishes react to heat or moisture, while some decorations need removal before refrigeration or serving.

Inspect the cake at handover

The collecting person or venue contact should inspect the order before accepting it. Ask about supports and non-edible decorations. After handover, the person transporting and storing the cake must protect it from heat, movement, and handling damage.

Include the handover area in your first message

Polite can quote the cake and delivery request together when you provide the event date, suburb, address type, and preferred handover time.
